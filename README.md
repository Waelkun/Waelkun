## 🚀 Things I've built

### 🥗 [Adasa (عدسة)](https://github.com/Waelkun/ADASA) · [Live demo](https://adasa-qomra.vercel.app/)
A nutrition and calorie tracker powered by vision AI. Snap a photo of your plate or a nutrition label and get calories, macros, and serving sizes in seconds. It also has zigzag calorie cycling, workout logging, and a polished dark mode that works on any screen.
`Next.js 15` `React 19` `TypeScript` `Tailwind v4` `Supabase` `GSAP`

### ⏱️ [Tanzeem (تنظيم)](https://github.com/Waelkun/tanzeem) · [Live demo](https://tanzeem-platform.vercel.app)
An AI-powered productivity hub with a Pomodoro timer that syncs live across devices. Start it on your phone, see it on your laptop, and it never drifts, even if you close the tab. An AI planner (Llama 3.3 70B) plans your day but only acts when you confirm.
`React` `Vite` `Supabase Realtime` `NVIDIA NIM` `Google Calendar API` `PWA`

### 🧠 [Headache Tracker](https://github.com/Waelkun/headache-tracker) · [Live demo](https://headache-tracker-platform.vercel.app/)
A pain and migraine diary with an interactive 3D head model: rotate it, tap the exact spot, log the intensity. It's fully bilingual (English and Arabic RTL) and exports a clean PDF report for doctor visits.
`React` `Three.js` `React Three Fiber` `Recharts` `Supabase`

### 🧾 [Fawatir (فواتير)](https://github.com/Waelkun/fawatir)
A smart receipt and expense manager. Scan a receipt, and Gemini AI extracts the store, tax, date, and every line item in Arabic or English. It includes a debt ledger and price history per product across merchants.
`React Native` `Expo` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Gemini`
